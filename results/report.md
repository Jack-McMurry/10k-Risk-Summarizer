## Analysis

**1. Shared risks**

- **Cybersecurity** appears for all 20 companies, almost always rated 4 or 5. It is the most universal risk in the set.
- **Regulatory and compliance costs** appear in nearly every company, though the specific rules differ by sector.
- **Operational and third-party failures** (outages, vendor breakdowns) appear in most companies.
- **Macroeconomic downturns** appear across all three sectors, but each sector transmits them differently.

**2. Sector differences**

- **Asset Management & Markets** (BLK, CME, MORN, SCHW, TROW) is driven by fee revenue tied to market levels, trading volumes, and client flows. Competition and fee compression are central. Average severity is about 3.7 to 4.3.
